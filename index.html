Editing. Tap an item to open it. Change its name, detail, price, category, when it's needed, and status right there. The coloured chip is a dropdown — change status without opening. Ticking the box marks a buy as bought or a task as done.

Prices & total. Type a number in an item's price box. The four totals at the top add up automatically — spent, still-to-buy, optional, and everything priced. Items with no price are just left out of the sums.

Saving. Everything lives in this browser. Use Backup before switching phone or browser and Restore on the other device. To share one link that opens anywhere, drop this file on app.netlify.com/drop or a GitHub Pages repo, then "Add to Home Screen" on the phone.
